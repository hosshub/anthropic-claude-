# Tayyibat v1.4 — monetization redesign

**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Version:** 1.4.0 · **Status:** spec for approval

Switch from **paid-upfront** to **free download + premium**. Triggered by
8 app units / $56 sales in 30 days: the paywall at the store door is killing
acquisition before anyone sees the product.

## Economics that shape the design

Measured Gemini cost per image analysis ≈ **$0.0006**:

| Usage | Cost/user/month |
|---|---|
| 1 scan/day | $0.018 |
| 2 scans/day | $0.036 |
| 7/day (current cap) | $0.127 |

Everything except AI scanning is **zero marginal cost** — the guide, the
148-dish food bank, the 15-day program, fasting, history, Body Intelligence,
Apple Health. That is a genuinely useful free app, so the free tier can be
generous and still cost ~nothing. Only the AI needs metering.

Commission is **15%** (Small Business Program, confirmed from proceeds ratio).

## The split (⚠️ needs sign-off before UI work)

### Free — no account cost, drives installs, reviews, and word of mouth
- Full **guide** (all 10 sections), **meal guidebook**, **meal banks**
- Full **food bank** — 148 Egyptian/Arabic dishes, unlimited logging
- **15-day program**, **weekly prep**, **fasting tracker**
- **History, calendar, body response, Body Intelligence**
- **Apple Health** (steps/energy/sleep/weight + budget offset)
- **Logging streak**, re-log, history search
- **3 AI meal scans per week**
- **1 AI suggestion set per week**

### Premium
- **Unlimited AI meal scans** (fair-use 10/day server-side)
- **Unlimited suggestions**, meal-type filter
- **Weekly meal plans** + planned-vs-actual adherence
- Future: export, provider sharing

Rationale: gate the metered, magical, recurring-value features; give away
everything static. A free user still has a complete Tayyibat reference app —
which is what earns the reviews the listing currently lacks.

## Products (RevenueCat, entitlement `premium`)

| ID | Type | Suggested EG | Suggested AE | Suggested US |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tayyibat.premium.monthly` | auto-renewable | 149 EGP | 11 AED | $2.99 |
| `tayyibat.premium.annual` | auto-renewable, 7-day free trial | 749 EGP | 55 AED | $14.99 |
| `tayyibat.premium.lifetime` | non-consumable | 1,499 EGP | 110 AED | $29.99 |

- **Set per-territory prices manually.** Apple's auto-conversion is what
  produced 400 EGP. Egypt should sit near local streaming-service pricing.
- Lifetime exists because subscription resistance is high in EG/AE; it
  converts the segment that will never take a recurring charge.
- Annual carries the free trial — it is the highest-LTV path.

## Grandfathering existing buyers (non-negotiable)

8 people paid up to 400 EGP. They get **premium permanently, free**.

**Chosen mechanism: Supabase account age.** The app has always required an
account *and* was paid-only, so **any account created before the v1.4 release
timestamp belongs to someone who paid**. On launch, compare
`auth.currentUser.createdAt` against a hard-coded cutoff; if earlier, grant a
permanent local `grandfathered` entitlement and mirror it server-side.

Simpler and more reliable than parsing `original_application_version` from the
StoreKit receipt (which needs a native channel and an iOS 16+ API for the
modern path). Receipt checking can be added later as belt-and-braces.

Cutoff constant lives in one place and is covered by tests.

## Server-side enforcement (client gating alone is bypassable)

```
RevenueCat webhook → supabase/functions/rc-webhook → user_entitlements
analyze() reads user_entitlements → picks the cap → bump_usage(p_limit)
```

- New table `user_entitlements(user_id PK, tier, expires_at, updated_at)`.
- `analyze` currently hard-codes `DAILY_LIMIT = 7`. It will look up the tier:
  free → 3/**week**, premium → 10/day fair use, grandfathered → 10/day.
- Reuses the existing `bump_usage` / `refund_usage` RPCs; only the limit and
  the window change.

## UX principles for the paywall

- **Soft gates, not walls.** A free user who runs out of scans sees what the
  scan *would* have given them plus an upgrade card — never a dead end.
- The food bank is offered as the free alternative every time a scan is
  blocked ("log it from the food bank instead").
- Paywall shows the annual trial first, monthly second, lifetime third.
- Required by App Review: restore purchases, terms + privacy links, clear
  price/period/renewal text before purchase.

## Out of scope for v1.4
- Android/Play billing (iOS first; RevenueCat makes this additive later).
- Promo codes / referral loops.
- Family sharing.

## Risks
- **One-way door:** going free-to-download is effectively irreversible without
  angering users. Accepted — the current model is not working.
- **App Review scrutiny** on subscriptions is higher; the checklist above
  covers the usual rejection reasons.
- Trial abuse is negligible at this scale and cost.
