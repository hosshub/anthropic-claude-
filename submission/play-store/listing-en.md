## Full description
*4000 characters max*

```
Tayyibat is an Arabic-first mindful eating tracker. It helps you stay
consistent with a simple food system organized around three zones:
🟢 Green — the foundation of clean eating
🟡 Yellow — in moderation
🔴 Red — avoid

✦ Core features:
• Photograph a meal and get an instant AI analysis with per-item reasoning.
• Per-meal compliance score, daily average, and a 30-day trend chart.
• Log how your body feels after a meal (fullness, bloating, energy, sleep).
• "When in doubt" — a quick four-card recap of the system.

✦ Smart guide:
• Philosophy and golden rules.
• Eating map across three zones + explicit forbidden list.
• The Tayyibat plate (base format).
• Interactive 15-day program.
• Meal banks for breakfast / lunch / dinner / snacks with direct photo capture.
• Weekly prep checklist that auto-resets each Saturday.

✦ AI suggestions:
• Get a single meal idea right now, or a 7-day plan.

✦ Fasting:
• Reminders for White Days (13/14/15 Hijri) and Mondays/Thursdays.
• Log and review your fasting history.

✦ Daily reminders:
• Morning / afternoon / evening tips, dinner nudge, Saturday prep nudge —
  with smart anti-repeat across the tips bank.

✦ History & insights:
• Monthly color-coded calendar.
• Body Intelligence (avg satiety/bloating, best meals, sleep impact,
  zone share).

✦ Privacy:
• Data is local to your device.
• Photos are analyzed and not retained on our servers.
• No tracking, no ads.
• Account delete in-app.

⚠️ Medical disclaimer:
This is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose,
or replace a physician or dietitian. If you have a medical condition,
consult your doctor before changing your diet.

The app is Arabic-first with full right-to-left layout.
```
