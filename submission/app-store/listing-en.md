## Description
*4000 characters max*

```
Tayyibat is an Arabic-first mindful eating tracker (with full RTL layout).
It helps you stay consistent with a simple food system organized around
three zones:
🟢 Green — the foundation of clean eating
🟡 Yellow — in moderation
🔴 Red — avoid

✦ Smart analysis
Photograph any meal → get a compliance score with per-item reasoning,
each item tagged green / yellow / red. The daily score appears as a large
ring on the Today tab.

✦ Guide 01→09
Philosophy, golden rules, eating map (all three zones with all groups),
explicit forbidden list, the Tayyibat plate, interactive 15-day program,
meal banks, weekly prep, and common mistakes.

✦ Meal banks
Four banks (breakfast / lunch / dinner / snack). Each item shows its
zone dot, composition, and a note, with a "Photograph this meal" button
that drops you straight into capture.

✦ 15-day program
Intro + 4-phase preview → Start → progress ring, today's card with focus,
suggested meal, and tip. Auto-advances day by day.

✦ Body response
After any meal log how you felt: 5 questions (fullness, bloating,
energy, sleep, worth repeating). These power the "How does your body
respond?" section — avg satiety/bloating, most comforting meals, heaviest
meals, sleep impact distribution.

✦ "When in doubt"
A 🤔 floating button on Today — a golden 4-card recap plus quick actions.

✦ AI suggestions
Get a single mindful meal idea right now, or a 7-day plan (Saturday → Friday)
generated by AI that's pinned to the system's rules.

✦ Fasting
Reminders for the White Days (13/14/15 Hijri) and Mondays/Thursdays.
Log fasting days and review the history.

✦ Reminders
Morning / afternoon / evening tips, dinner nudge, Saturday prep reminder —
with smart anti-repeat across the last 10 tips shown per slot.

✦ Calendar & insights
Monthly color-coded calendar, Body Intelligence (last 30 days),
zone share in your plate, and a 30-day compliance trend line.

✦ Privacy
Local data (your meals, photos, notes) lives on your device. Analysis
photos pass through Google Gemini and are not retained on our servers.
No tracking, no ads. In-app account deletion wipes your server-side
account immediately.

⚠️ Medical disclaimer
This is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose,
or replace a physician or dietitian. With any medical condition,
pregnancy, or while breastfeeding, consult your doctor before changing
your diet.
```
